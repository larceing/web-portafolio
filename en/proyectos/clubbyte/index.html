Club Byte: technology explained to children aged 8 to 12

Problem
There is hardly any content in Spanish that explains to children how computing works through stories and characters, without selling a course behind it.
What I did
A YouTube channel and a blog with two main characters, Lía and Teo, and Bit, a robot made in Blender. Each episode explains one idea (sensors, conditionals, binary, algorithms) and ends with an unplugged challenge for home or class.
Result
A 3D animated intro, scripts, voices and the first episode, plus a blog for families and teachers with activity sheets. No cookies or tracking, as a children’s site should be.
