Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?
It depends on the pages and on what it has to do: a brochure site is not the same as one with bookings or a shop. Tell me what you need and I’ll send you a fixed quote in writing.
Do you use WordPress?
Not by default: I build custom sites, which are faster and need less security maintenance. If you already have WordPress and it works for you, we can look at it.
Where is it hosted?
On my server in Germany (EU), with HTTPS, nightly backups and continuous checks that it responds. I can also put it on your own hosting.
Can I change the text myself?
If you need to, the site comes with a simple panel to change text and photos; if not, ask me and I change it as part of monthly maintenance.
