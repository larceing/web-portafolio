Automate the orders that arrive by email

In many distributors and factories someone spends every morning copying orders from email into the ERP, line by line. It can be automated: the system reads the email and the PDF, recognises the customer and the products, checks prices and stock, and enters the order. It only asks you about what isn’t clear.

Ask for a quote

I reply within 24 working hours.

What’s included

Receive and sort

It reads the orders mailbox and separates orders from everything else: questions, invoices or advertising.

Extract

It takes customer, item codes, quantities and dates from the email body, a PDF or an attached Excel file.

Validate

It checks with your ERP that the customer and items exist, and flags prices, minimums or stock that don’t add up.

Enter

It enters the order in the ERP through its API or whatever route it supports, and keeps the original email linked.

Follow up

A review tray with anything doubtful and a daily summary of what came in, what was entered and what is waiting.

n8n, Python, AI (OpenAI or Claude), Microsoft 365 or Gmail, ERP API, SQL Server

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with PDF or scanned orders?
Yes, with text PDFs and most readable scans. Anything the system can’t read with confidence goes to the review tray instead of being entered.
What if it gets something wrong?
It never enters anything doubtful: if data is missing or doesn’t match the ERP, it asks you. Every entered order stays linked to its email so you can check it.
Which ERPs does it work with?
Those with an API or an accessible database. If your ERP only imports files, the import file is prepared for it.
How much does it cost?
It depends on how many order formats you receive and how your ERP connects. Tell me about your case and I’ll send you a fixed written quote, with no obligation.
