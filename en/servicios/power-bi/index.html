Frequently asked questions

How much does a Power BI dashboard cost?
It depends on the data sources, the number of pages and the calculations. Tell me about your case and I’ll send you a fixed quote in writing, with no obligation.
What is an analysis pack?
A set of Power BI pages designed for one area (sales, plant, logistics or quality) with its indicators already defined. I connect it to your data and adjust it to the way you work, so you don’t start from scratch. Tell me which packs interest you and I’ll send you a fixed quote.
Do I need a Power BI licence?
To build and view the report on your computer, Power BI Desktop is enough and it is free. To share it online with your team you need Power BI Pro, which is paid to Microsoft per user.
Does it work with my ERP?
I have worked with A3 ERP, SAP, Clavei, Cybermax and SQL Server. If your ERP stores its data in a database or exports it to Excel, it can be connected.
Can I change it myself later?
Yes. I hand it over documented and explain how it is built. If you would rather not touch it, there is monthly maintenance.
