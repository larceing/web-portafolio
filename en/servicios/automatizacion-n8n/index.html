Frequently asked questions

What is n8n?
n8n is an automation tool that connects programs with visual flows. It can be installed on your own server, so your data stays in the EU.
Which tasks can be automated?
The ones that repeat and always follow the same steps: copying data between programs, sending alerts or emails, generating reports or PDFs, checking forms and sorting messages.
How much does an automation cost?
It depends on how many applications are connected and on the special cases. Tell me about your case and I’ll send you a fixed quote in writing.
What if a program changes?
The flow warns you when something stops working and gets adjusted. With monthly maintenance, I do it.
