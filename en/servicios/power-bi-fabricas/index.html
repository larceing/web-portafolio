Power BI for factories: OEE, downtime and scrap

OEE measures how much of a machine’s time turns into good parts: availability × performance × quality. With Power BI you see it every morning by machine, line and shift, next to the downtime and scrap that pull it down, without building the Excel by hand.

Frequently asked questions

What is OEE and how is it calculated?
OEE (overall equipment effectiveness) is availability × performance × quality. If a machine was stopped for 10% of planned time, ran at 90% of its ideal speed and 95% of its parts were good, its OEE is 0.9 × 0.9 × 0.95 = 77%.
Do I need a MES or new sensors?
Not always. You can start with what the ERP or the production reports already record. If you want live machine data, it is read from the PLC or SCADA in read-only mode.
Does it work for small factories?
Yes. It is designed for manufacturing SMEs with a few lines, where OEE is often worked out by hand in Excel once a month.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the machines, where the data comes from and how many packs you want. Tell me about your plant and I’ll send you a fixed written quote, with no obligation.
